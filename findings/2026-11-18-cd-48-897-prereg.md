# Pre-registration: TESS eclipse fits of CD-48 897, TOI-710 and UCAC4 117-030318

2026-11-18. MV2 of Munim, Inc.; made with AI agents. Text CC BY 4.0. Code `eb_char.py`
0.1.0 and `eb_ephem.py` 0.1.0 (Apache-2.0). Config `data/ebchar-v1/targets.json`. Job
`gaia-ebchar-v1`.

**What we have already seen.** We have seen the v4 search's box events for these stars
(`findings/2026-11-18-xmatch-results.md`), so this is not a blind test of whether they
eclipse. What is fixed here, before any eclipse fit exists, is the set of rules that
decides whether CD-48 897 is reported to AAVSO VSX, and our expectations for the
secondary eclipse and the other two systems.

## Method (fixed)

1. **Light curves.** All TESS light curves, one per sector (SPOC > TESS-SPOC > QLP). Each
   segment is divided by a running median (1.5 d window for CD-48 897) that ignores
   ±0.3 d around each predicted primary eclipse, and ±0.8 d around the Gaia-predicted
   other conjunction.
2. **Eclipse fits.** Each predicted primary eclipse with data gets a trapezoid with a
   local linear baseline, fitted by least squares from 12 starting points. Free
   parameters: mid-time, depth, total duration, ingress fraction.
   - Errors come from the Jacobian, scaled by the residual scatter.
   - An eclipse is "good" if both halves have at least 3 points, more than 50% of the
     expected in-eclipse points are present, depth > max(5σ, 0.2%), and its timing error
     is finite.
3. **Ephemeris.** A weighted linear ephemeris from the good eclipses, with timing errors
   floored at 1 minute.
4. **Secondary eclipse.** A box scan at phases 0.02-0.98 (widths 0.1, 0.2, 0.35 and
   0.5 d) outside the primary windows. The best boxes are reported overall and within
   ±0.04 in phase of the Gaia-predicted other conjunction (phase 0.7398 after the
   primary).
5. **Dip census.** Every 30-min bin deeper than max(0.5%, 5σ) is listed.

## Criteria for reporting CD-48 897 to VSX (`eb_ephem.vsx_criteria`)

- **E1.** At least 4 good primary eclipses deeper than 5%.
- **E2.** Ephemeris rms O − C < 30 min, and |P_fit − P_Gaia| ≤ 3σ_Gaia (P_Gaia =
  26.53686 ± 0.00342 d).
- **E3.** (max − min) / median of the fitted depths ≤ 0.25.
- **E4.** The shallowest depth > 3 × the TIC contamination ratio (0.021).

If E1-E4 all pass, CD-48 897 is reported to VSX as an EA (detached) eclipsing binary
with the fitted ephemeris, the TESS-band depth and the Gaia DR3 cross-identification. If
any fails, we report why and do not submit.

**Our expectations:**
- E1-E4 all pass: 85%.
- The fitted period lies within 0.002 d of 26.5328: 80%.
- **S1.** No significant secondary eclipse (SNR < 7) within ±0.04 of phase 0.7398: 60%.
  A grazing primary at an eccentric orbit's nearer conjunction often means no eclipse at
  the farther one. Gaia predicts the other conjunction lasts 8.6 h against 4.6 h, so it
  is the farther one.
- **S2.** If a secondary is found, its depth is ≤ 3% (an early M dwarf's surface
  brightness in the TESS band is about 0.2 of a G dwarf's): 90% conditional.

## TOI-710.01 (HD 70623, TIC 309402106)

The TOI ephemeris is T0 = BTJD 1343.942146 and P = 216.24584 d. The fit uses P/3 =
72.08195 d.
- **T1.** At least one good eclipse falls at a cycle n with n mod 3 ≠ 0 (a time the TOI
  period does not predict). If so, the period is ≤ 72.08 d. Expectation: 90%. (We have
  already seen three events spaced ~72.08 d apart in the v4 event list.)
- **T2.** The fitted period agrees with Gaia's SB1 period (71.931 ± 0.054 d) within 3σ.
  Expectation: 50%. 72.082 is 2.8σ from it before any fit.

## UCAC4 117-030318 (TIC 344750398)

- **U1.** The Sector 90 event at BTJD 3756.49 is re-fitted with depth > 5% and duration
  4-12 h: 70%.
- **U2.** No other predicted epoch (P = 193.04 d) has TESS data: 80%. A covered epoch
  without an eclipse would argue against the event being on this star.

## Not decided by this job

The companion's radius (grazing geometry), the spectroscopic K from the ground, and a
pixel-level centroid test. The VSX report rests on E1-E4 plus the Gaia orbit at the same
period, and on E4's contamination argument.
