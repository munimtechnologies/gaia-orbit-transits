## The star

TIC 93622019 = Gaia DR3 5345417757181174144: G = 12.57, BP-RP = 0.715, Gaia Teff 6075 K (TIC
6502 K, R = 1.36 R_sun), RUWE 0.90. TESS: P = 2.1238933 d, T0 = BTJD 3706.08685, deblended depth
1.28% (about 1.5 R_Jup), found by our Gaia-transit work with Gaia's period wrong by a 4:3 alias
(`findings/2026-11-24-vpt-epochs-box-results.md`). Gaia DR3 radial-velocity scatter limits a
companion to < 0.10 M_sun at 95% (`findings/2026-11-24-tic93622019-gaia-rv-limit.md`). On ExoFOP
(read 25 Nov 2026 ~07:40 UTC) it still has no TOI, no CTOI and no observing notes.

Gaia DR3 sources within 45": the nearest is 2.51" away at G = 19.56 (7.0 mag fainter, 0.16% of
the light; even a total eclipse of it could not make a 1.3% dip). The next is 8.0" away at G = 20.8.
Gaia's own G photometry measures the target in a window about 0.7" x 2.1" (BP/RP 3.5" x 2.1"), so
TESS's neighbours do not enter it.

## What it means

1. **The eclipsed source is not redder than the target.** In the small-light limit the BP/RP depth
   ratio is 10^(-0.4 dC), dC = colour of the eclipsed source minus the target's. The 2-sigma range
   0.94-1.69 allows dC from -0.57 to +0.07 mag. An eclipse on an unresolved K dwarf (BP-RP about
   1.2, ratio about 0.63) is excluded at about 3.6 sigma, and on an M dwarf (BP-RP about 2.5, ratio
   about 0.19) at about 6 sigma.
2. A ratio a little above 1 is what limb darkening gives for a transit across the target itself; the
   1.9-sigma excess is driven mostly by the one flagged epoch.
3. With the earlier checks this leaves few false-positive routes: an unresolved source of the same
   colour or bluer within about 0.5-1", or a companion that is itself colour-neutral. A stellar
   companion above 0.10 M_sun is already excluded by Gaia's RV scatter, and the 1.5 R_Jup radius is
   large for anything between a planet and 0.10 M_sun unless it is young. **TIC 93622019 is now
   the candidate with the most independent checks passed of our three Gaia transit candidates**
   (TIC 44997521: colour-neutral too, no RV bound; TIC 4638108: inconclusive, leans stellar).

## Limits

Four in-transit epochs; hand-chosen windows (fixed on the first two stars, then applied unchanged);
error bars from out-of-transit scatter. The colour argument assumes the eclipsed source adds little
light. The G out-of-transit series has several ~1% low points (e.g. Gaia time 2409.016, 2202.824),
so single-epoch depths are uncertain at that level. Exploratory, not pre-registered.
