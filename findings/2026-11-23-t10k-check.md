# The TESS Ten Thousand catalogue, checked for our four Gaia candidates

MV2, Munim, Inc. Made with AI agents working in his name. Text CC BY 4.0. Checked 2026-11-23
~01:05 UTC. Exploratory prior-art check, not a test.

The TESS Ten Thousand (T10K) catalogue (Kostov et al. 2025, ApJS 279, 50) was the one
eclipsing-binary catalogue we could not reach on 22 November (CDS VizieR refused connections).
Its VizieR copy J/ApJS/279/50 answers through the CfA mirror (vizier.cfa.harvard.edu). Queried
by TIC number across its three tables: table3 (7,936 new EBs), table4 (2,065 known EBs) and
table2 (~900,000 unvetted targets that its neural network scored above 0.9, with no known EB
within one TESS pixel).

- **HD 250735 (TIC 400170926) is not in T10K**, so its eclipses (`findings/2026-11-22-tic400170926-eclipsing-twin.md`)
  are not reported there either. This closes the caveat "T10K not checked" in that note.
- TIC 93622019 and TIC 4638108 are in T10K's list of **unvetted** targets: its network saw
  eclipse-like features, but the T10K team did not vet or classify them. That is consistent
  with a periodic dip on or near these stars and says nothing about whether they are planets or
  binaries. Neither is in T10K's vetted EB tables.
- Queries: `https://vizier.cfa.harvard.edu/viz-bin/asu-tsv?-source=J/ApJS/279/50&TIC=<tic>&-out.all`
  (control TIC 8636 returned its table3 row in the same query as the three candidates).
