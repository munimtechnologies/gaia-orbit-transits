# Pre-registration: TESS check of HD 11239, the one new eclipsing candidate of the blind Gaia search

MV2, Munim, Inc. Made with AI agents working in his name; no human expert has checked it yet.
Text CC BY 4.0; code Apache-2.0. Written 2026-11-26 ~04:00 UTC, **before** job `hd11239-tess-v1`
ran and before any TESS light curve of HD 11239 was looked at by us.

## Why
The pre-registered blind run of rule g8 on all 927 Gaia DR3 double-lined orbits with epoch
photometry (findings/2026-11-26-g8-dr3-blind-results.md) flagged 22 stars that Gaia itself does
not call eclipsing. Hand vetting found 21 of them already listed as eclipsing elsewhere (or found
earlier by MV2). Only **HD 11239 = TIC 115321276 = Gaia DR3 4961129715887496320** (G 9.58, F-type,
Gaia SB2 P = 3.1208264 d, e = 0.004, M sin^3 i = 1.09 and 1.05 Msun) is listed as eclipsing nowhere
we could reach, and it has no TESS SPOC TCE (it was not a 2-minute target). Gaia's own photometry
shows three faint epochs (0.086, 0.054 and 0.041 mag), one at one conjunction of the Gaia orbit
(dphase -0.003) and two at the other (+0.010, +0.016), from three different orbits.

## Job (code unchanged)
`vpt_followup.py` 0.1.0 at commit 0d8d4c5 (period-free BLS over all TESS sectors from SPOC,
TESS-SPOC or QLP; fine scans at Gaia's P, 2P and P/2), with the same pinned helper modules as the
graded job vpt-followup-v1. Targets: fu_targets.csv in the job folder. Two positive controls are
known double-lined eclipsing binaries flagged by the same blind run: BN Scl (Prsa et al. 2022,
P = 3.6505249 d) and HD 157949 (VSX/ASAS ED, P = 3.041 d). CD-28 7251 (MV2's earlier find, whose
TESS pipeline fit did not converge) is run as an exploratory target with no prediction.

Predicted Gaia conjunctions of HD 11239 (hand, from Gaia's orbit; Gaia time = BTJD + 1802.5):
conjunction 0 at BTJD 389.2946 + n x 3.1208264, conjunction 1 at BTJD 390.8619 + n x 3.1208264.

## Predictions (graded by hand from out/fu_summary.json)
| # | Prediction | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| C | Both controls: combined S/N >= 7 and TESS period within 0.1% of the catalogue period or half of it | 85% |
| T1 | HD 11239: combined S/N >= 7 and TESS period within 1% of Gaia's P or P/2 | 70% |
| T2 | HD 11239: the TESS epoch t0 lies within +-0.109 d (0.035 of Gaia's orbit) of a predicted conjunction, propagated with Gaia's P | 60% |
| T3 | HD 11239: TESS depth at that period >= 1% | 60% |

If C fails, T1-T3 are not graded. If T1-T3 hold, HD 11239 is reported as a new double-lined
eclipsing binary (Gaia masses x TESS eclipses) and queued for the AAVSO VSX index. If T1 fails, the
Gaia-only flag is reported as unconfirmed and the blind search's measured purity is revised.
A data-access failure is re-filed unchanged; a code failure is fixed and the version bumped.
