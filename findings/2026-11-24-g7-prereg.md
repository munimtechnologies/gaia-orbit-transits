# Pre-registration, step g7 (Gaia DR4 day): eclipse-locked fits of Gaia's per-transit double-lined velocities

MV2, Munim, Inc. Made with AI agents working in his name. Text CC BY 4.0, code Apache-2.0.
Written 2026-11-24 (~06:30 UTC), eight days before Gaia DR4 (2 Dec 2026, 12:00 CET). No DR4 data
exist yet. Code: `sb2_rvfit.py` 0.1.0 with `test_sb2_rvfit.py` (stdlib, unit tests and built-in
self-test), runner `runbook/g7/g7_run.py` 0.1.0, inputs `runbook/g7/targets.json` (this commit).

## Why

Gaia DR3 published only fitted orbits for its double-lined binaries. DR4 publishes the velocities
behind them: `rvs_epoch_parameters_double`, the per-transit radial velocities of both stars for
transits identified as double-lined (787,312 records, DataLink only; ESA DR4 content page,
cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dr4, chapter 17.01). For a binary that also eclipses in TESS, the eclipses
fix the period and the conjunction time far better than Gaia's sparse spectra can. With those
fixed, the velocities are a linear problem in gamma, K1 and K2, and we can test from outside the
Gaia pipeline:
1. whether Gaia's velocities put conjunction where the eclipses are (a timing check of the whole
   chain: Gaia's clock, component assignment and line blending);
2. whether our K1, K2 agree with Gaia DR4's own orbit; and
3. what absolute masses the three new eclipsing binaries found in this project have (HD 250735,
   CD-28 7251, TYC 3616-2395-1; `findings/2026-11-23-sb2-screen-results.md`). The masses use the
   inclinations from the queued eclipse fit (sb2-ebfit-v1).

HD 250735 adds one specific question. Its two Gaia DR3 photometric points in eclipse and its DR3
orbit's conjunction both sit earlier than the TESS ephemeris extrapolated back to 2014-2017
(`findings/2026-11-24-hd250735-gaia-epochs.md`, exploratory, about 2σ). Per-transit velocities over
66 months settle it.

## Method (fixed now)

- **Inputs per star** (`runbook/g7/targets.json`): P, e, ω (Gaia's, component 1), an eclipse time
  t_conj, ephemeris error eph_sigma, K priors (only to seed the component assignment), inclination
  from the eclipse fit. **Rule for the FILL fields, applied on DR4 day before any DR4 velocity is
  read:** t_conj, P and eph_sigma come from sb2-new-ebs-timing-v1 if it ended CONTROLS_OK, else from
  the SPOC multi-sector TCE on exo.MAST, with eph_sigma = 0.005 d. The inclination and its error come from sb2-ebfit-v1 if
  it ended CONTROLS_OK, else none (minimum masses only, and the mass grades are not made).
- **DR3 → DR4 identifiers** through `gaiadr4.dr3_neighbourhood` (nearest candidate), as in step g6.
- **Velocities:** DataLink `EPOCH_PARAMETERS_RVS_DOUBLE`, CSV. Columns are found by name
  (`map_columns`). If time, both velocities and both errors cannot be identified, the star is
  SCHEMA_UNKNOWN. If no star can be read, the job exits 4 and the column names are recorded for an
  amendment. Gaia times (BJD_TCB − 2455197.5) are converted to BTJD (−1802.5).
- **Fit** (`sb2_rvfit`): v1 = γ + K1 f(t), v2 = γ − K2 f(t), f = cos(ν+ω) + e cos ω, with the
  periastron time tied to t_conj + dt. Epochs whose two observed velocities are within 20 km/s are
  dropped as blended. The two velocities of each epoch are assigned to the two stars by the better
  fit, iterated to stability, and swaps are counted. dt is scanned over ±0.05 P (401 steps) for
  both possible conjunction identities (Gaia's component 1 or 2 behind at t_conj). The lower χ² wins.
  Errors are scaled by sqrt(χ²/dof) when that exceeds 1. The dt interval is Δχ² ≤ that same scale.
- **Grades per star:**
  - **C (conjunction):** CONJ_AGREES if |dt| ≤ 3 sqrt(σ_dt² + eph_sigma²), else CONJ_EARLY or
    CONJ_LATE. UNCONSTRAINED if the interval reaches the scan edge.
  - **K (vs Gaia DR4 orbit):** K_OK if both |z| ≤ 3 against DR4 `nss_two_body_orbit` (the better
    of the two component mappings).
  - **M (controls only, needs inclination):** M_OK if both masses are within 3σ of the literature.
- **Controls** (the four literature eclipsing binaries of sb2-ebfit, fixed on 23 Nov before any
  fit): ZZ Boo, CW Eri, EY Cep, BK Peg. **CONTROLS_OK** if at least 3 of 4 have C = CONJ_AGREES,
  K_OK and (where graded) M_OK. If the controls fail, the targets' grades are reported but not
  interpreted.
- **Fewer than 6 usable epochs:** TOO_FEW, not graded.

## What follows

- **g7b (exploratory, same day):** the same fit for every new TESS-eclipsing double-lined binary
  that step g5 finds in DR4. If the controls pass, this gives a list of new eclipsing binaries with
  absolute masses from Gaia's velocities and TESS's eclipses, the main payoff of this line of work.
- HD 250735: if C = CONJ_EARLY with the controls passing, the eclipse timing is followed up with
  DR4 epoch photometry and all TESS sectors (a third body or a period change). If CONJ_AGREES, the
  DR3 hint was noise and we say so.
- Job template: `runbook/g7/README.md`.

## Limits

The DR4 table's column names are not public yet (the draft data model is a zip we could not open
here), so the column mapping is a guess backed by a gate and controls. Blended epochs are dropped,
not modelled. The fit fixes e and ω at Gaia's values. Per-transit velocities of double-lined stars
may carry template-mismatch biases that a fixed-shape fit cannot remove. The controls test this only
at the 3σ level. This work was carried out with AI agents and no human expert has checked it yet.
