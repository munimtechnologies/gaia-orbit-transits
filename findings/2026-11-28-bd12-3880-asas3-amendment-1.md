# Amendment 1 to the BD+12 3880 ASAS-3 pre-registration

Written 2026-11-28 ~07:20 UTC, after the SIMBAD and VizieR lookups named in the pre-registration and BEFORE the ASAS-3 light curve was requested (the request was issued in the same step as this file, so nothing in this amendment depends on the photometry).

## What the lookups showed

1. **V1208 Aql (28 Aql, HD 181333) is V = 5.52, F0III, a low-amplitude delta Scuti star (GCVS DSCTC, P = 0.149663 d, 5.51-5.56 V), 60.4 arcsec from the target.** The pre-registration's trigger ("brighter than V = 8") fires. It is 3.5 mag (about 25 times) brighter than BD+12 3880 and will saturate ASAS-3's wide-field CCD, so charge bleeding and PSF wings may reach the target's aperture.
2. **BD+12 3880 is already in VSX** as "Gaia DR3 4318964656978448768", type VAR, 8.880-9.010 G, period 0.99786 d (Gaia DR3 variability classification), and in the TESS OBAF catalogue (IJspeert et al. 2024) as "sine_like 1.0, score 0.0". 0.99786 d is half the Gaia orbital period (1.99580 d / 2 = 0.99790 d), exactly what an ellipsoidal double wave gives a period search. So the *variability* and its half-period are known; the *eclipse at conj0* and the identification of the star as a binary that dims at Gaia's conjunction are not (it is absent from every eclipsing-binary table in the cone: Prsa et al. 2022, Kostov et al. 2025 tables 3-4, IJspeert et al. 2021 and 2024 EB lists, GCVS, ASAS-3 variables).

## Changes (made before the data)

- **Primary aperture is MAG_0** (2-pixel, ~30 arcsec diameter), the smallest, to keep V1208 Aql's core 60 arcsec away out of the aperture; MAG_1 is the check aperture. Everything else about point selection is unchanged (GRADE A or B, 29.999 excluded).
- **Data-quality gate, applied before any prediction is graded:** if fewer than half of the A/B points lie within 0.06 mag of the median MAG_0 (i.e. the scatter is far above the ~0.03 mag expected at V 9), the light curve is declared contaminated and the whole test is INCONCLUSIVE; no predictions are graded.
- **Interpretation of A1 and A2 is downgraded**: because the half-period double wave is already catalogued, A1 and A2 passing confirms only that Gaia's orbital ephemeris phases the known variability correctly back to 2000-2009 (a timing result, useful but not new variability). **A3 (faint points at conj0 at least twice those at conj1) is the discriminating prediction**: pure ellipsoidal variation gives equal minima, an eclipse at conj0 does not. A3 PASS with A1 and A2 PASS would support the eclipse reading; A3 FAIL with A1 and A2 PASS would say ASAS-3 sees only the ellipsoidal wave (the Gaia dips at conj0 then rest on Gaia's seven epochs alone until TESS).
- A4 and all thresholds, windows, confidences and verdict rules are unchanged.

## Not changed

No TESS or other photometry is consulted. The queued TESS test (sb1-tier2-tess-v1, predictions L1-L3) remains the decisive test for the eclipse.
