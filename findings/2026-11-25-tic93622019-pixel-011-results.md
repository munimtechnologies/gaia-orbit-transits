## TIC 93622019

- PSF-fit difference images put the transit on the target in all 6 sensitive sectors (10, 37,
  64, 90, 99, 100; 66 transits). Free-position centroids are on target in 5 of 6; sector 99 gives
  CENTROID_ELSEWHERE (4.0 sigma in pixel units, 0.61 px), while its PSF fit still says target.
- Odd/even depths 1.65% +- 0.16% vs 1.57% +- 0.17% (z = 0.33); no secondary at phase 0.5
  (0.08% +- 0.23%). TESS refined ephemeris: P = 2.12389333 +- 0.00000304 d, T0 = BTJD
  3706.08685 +- 0.00068 (5 sector epochs, chi2_red 1.39).
- **WS_UNRESOLVED is caused by one source only:** Gaia DR3 5345417757181557248, 2.40" away, which
  the TESS fit merges with the target (M1). As fixed in amendment 1, that source is judged by Gaia's
  own photometry. Gaia DR3 (AIP TAP, read 25 Nov 2026 ~10:40 UTC): G = 19.559 against the
  target's 12.569 (6.99 mag, flux ratio 0.0016), BP/RP excess factor 10.0, negative parallax,
  ipd_frac_multi_peak 8: a faint source whose BP/RP are dominated by the target's light. Gaia's
  own G epochs of the target show the transit at the TESS times (1.37% mean in-transit depth,
  `findings/2026-11-25-tic93622019-bp-rp-colour.md`). To make that from the neighbour, even with
  all its light inside the target's window, its own eclipse would have to be 0.0137 x 626 = 8.6
  times its total flux, which is impossible; any eclipse up to 100% deep needs a source no more
  than 4.64 mag fainter than the target (G <= 17.2). And the target's Gaia G window (at most
  about 1" x 2.1") does not reach 2.4".
- **Verdict under the pre-registered rules: the 2.124-day transit is on TIC 93622019** (to the
  arcsecond-level resolution of Gaia; a bound companion closer than ~0.5-1" that has the target's
  colour is not excluded by these tests).

0.10 M_sun in Gaia's RVs, equal depth in Gaia BP and RP, and now the transit located on the

## Limits

Gaussian PSF, not the TESS PRF; Gaia RP stands in for the TESS band in the merge weights. The
neighbour argument uses Gaia's G for a source whose photometry is contaminated by the target (the
true source can only be fainter, which strengthens it). A candidate, not a planet.
