# Pre-registration: vetting the TIC 41898425 dip (job gaia-vet-41898425-v1)

Written 2026-11-17, before the job ran. By MV2 of Munim, Inc. Made with AI agents
working in his name. CC BY 4.0. Code: vet_event.py 0.1.0 (gaia-orbit-transits).

## The event
- Star: TIC 41898425 = Gaia DR3 4984264505688458112, a K0 dwarf (R = 0.93 R☉).
- Event: TESS Sector 69 (TESS-SPOC), BTJD 3192.263, 8-h box, ~2,100 ppm, SNR 16. It ends
  0.15 d before the end of orbit 1's data.
- Gaia orbit: P = 196.674 ± 0.817 d, companion ≈ 0.076 M☉.
- Expected central transit: depth ≈ 1.05% ((0.095/0.93)²), duration ≈ 11.7 h.

## What the job measures
1. Every light-curve product of the target that covers the event: PDC or detrended flux,
   SAP flux, background, both centroids and the quality flags. Each is measured in the
   same 8-h box against a straight-line baseline fitted within 1.5 d (the baseline is
   one-sided here because the event ends at the data gap).
2. The same box on the 15 nearest other SPOC / TESS-SPOC stars observed in Sector 69.
3. A TESScut 15×15 stamp: a background-subtracted 3×3 aperture light curve and a
   difference image (out-of-box mean minus in-box mean), with the centroid of the
   difference image within 3 px of the target.
4. A positive control: a 1.05%, 11.7-h box injected at the event time and 3 and 6 d
   earlier must be recovered by the same measurement.
5. Repeat epochs t0 + kP for P within ±3σ, in every covered sector. Cadences below half
   the median flux are dropped (the Sector 104 QLP light curve is broken at orbit edges).
   For each k it records the covered period range, the best box SNR, and the minimum SNR
   of an injected 1.05% transit.
6. Catalogues: a Gaia DR3 cone of 63″ (G < 18.4), SIMBAD within 60″, and VSX within 120″.

## Fixed decision rules (vet_event.verdict)
- **REJECTED (systematic)** if any of these hold:
  - ≥ 30% of the neighbour stars dim at > 3σ in the same box;
  - the dip is in PDC (SNR ≥ 3) but missing from SAP (SNR < 3 and depth < 1/3 of PDC's);
  - more than half the in-box cadences carry quality flags.
- **REJECTED (off-target)** if the difference-image centroid is more than 1 pixel from the
  target and the 3×3-aperture dip has SNR ≥ 3.
- **REJECTED (not substellar)** if the depth is above 3%.
- **REJECTED (not significant)** if the PDC box SNR is below 3 against the local
  baseline.
- **UNRESOLVED** if no rejection holds but either:
  - less than half the box has data; or
  - a repeat epoch that covers ≥ 90% of the ±3σ period range, where an injected transit
    would give SNR ≥ 7, shows SNR < 3. That rules out the period, not the event, because
    Gaia's period could be off by more than 3σ.
- **SURVIVES** otherwise. A surviving event is still only a single-transit candidate.
  Before any report to TFOP or ExoFOP it needs:
  - the Gaia-period-folded repeat check;
  - archival ground photometry (gaia-archival-v2 covers ZTF, ASAS-SN and Gaia epochs);
  - a human look at the pixels.

## Predictions
- **V1.** Most likely outcome (my prior, about 85%): REJECTED as systematic. The dip sits
  at the end of an orbit, right after a rise in flux, where scattered light is strongest.
  The Gaia transit probability is only ~1%.
- **V2.** The Sector 104 repeat epoch (k = 5) covers only part of the ±3σ period range,
  because the k = 5 window spans BTJD 4163–4188 and Sector 104 starts near 4178.6.
- **V3.** The injection control at t0 − 3 d gives SNR ≥ 7 for a 1.05% box: Sector 69 is
  sensitive to a full transit of this companion.
